# Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from our clients and team members.

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="How long does it typically take to design and launch a new custom website?">
    Standard high-converting business websites take 2 to 3 weeks from wireframe sign-off to live production deployment. More complex eCommerce stores or custom web applications take 4 to 6 weeks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do payments work for Nexium Media services?">
    Payments for website development and monthly marketing packages are processed securely via Razorpay or direct bank transfer. Milestones are structured into an initial deposit upon project kickoff, staging milestone review, and final settlement upon domain handover.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How soon can we expect to see organic SEO results on Google?">
    Local search improvements (such as Google Map Pack rankings in Dhanbad and regional queries) typically become visible within 4 to 6 weeks. Broad competitive organic keywords usually establish authority within 3 to 6 months of continuous optimization and backlinking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do team members contribute new documentation pages?">
    Create a new `.mdx` file in `content/docs/[category]/` and push a pull request! Fumadocs automatically detects your new page and adds it to the search index and sidebar navigation.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
