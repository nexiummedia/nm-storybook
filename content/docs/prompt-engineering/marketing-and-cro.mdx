# Marketing & Performance Copywriting Prompts

At Nexium Media, marketing performance is measured strictly by conversion rate optimization (CRO), return on ad spend (ROAS), and customer acquisition efficiency. Vague prompts (*"Write 5 catchy Facebook ads for our web design agency"*) consistently generate generic, AI-sounding copy laden with trite cliches like *"revolutionary"*, *"game-changer"*, and *"unleash your potential"*.

High-converting generative copy requires structured psychological frameworks, explicit persona anchoring, strict negative constraints, and few-shot brand calibration.

---

## The C-P-T-C-F Marketing Prompt Framework

Every high-performing performance marketing prompt incorporates five modular layers:

```text
1. Context: Product unique selling proposition (USP), pricing tier, target audience psychographics, and competitor positioning.
2. Persona: The exact professional capability (e.g. "Senior Direct-Response Copywriter & CRO Strategist").
3. Task: The explicit asset to generate (e.g. Meta feed ads, Google RSAs, Landing Page Hero section).
4. Constraints: Banned buzzwords, strict character limits, reading grade level, compliance rules.
5. Format: The exact structural schema (e.g. Markdown comparison matrix with hooks, body, CTA).
```

## 1. High-ROAS Multi-Angle Ad Copy Engine

Performance media buyers test ad creatives across diverse psychological angles to identify winning message-market fit. This prompt generates 5 distinct angles based on proven direct-response triggers:

```markdown
# Role & Objective
Act as a Senior Direct-Response Copywriter specializing in high-ROAS paid social ads (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn).

# Campaign Context
- Product/Service: Nexium Media High-Performance Web Development for B2B SaaS
- Target Audience: B2B Founders, CTOs, and Heads of Growth experiencing high bounce rates (>70%) on legacy WordPress/Webflow sites.
- Core Value Proposition: Custom Next.js web applications engineered for sub-second load times, 95+ Core Web Vitals, and 2.4x higher pipeline conversion.
- Average Contract Value: $15,000 – $40,000.

# Task
Generate 5 distinct ad copy variations. Each variation must test a unique psychological angle:
1. Pain-Point Agitation (Focus on slow load speeds hemorrhaging paid ad spend).
2. Social Proof / Authority (Focus on empirical conversion lift and engineering benchmarks).
3. Curiosity / Pattern Interrupt (Challenge the consensus that design aesthetics matter more than latency).
4. Aspirational / Identity (Position the brand as an enterprise category leader).
5. Loss Aversion / Financial Risk (Quantify the exact revenue lost per second of page delay).

# Negative Constraints (Strict Guardrails)
- Banned AI words: "revolutionary", "game-changer", "unleash", "skyrocket", "delve", "seamless", "tapestry".
- Never use exclamation marks in primary text.
- Primary text must place the hook within the first 125 characters (before the "See more" fold).
- Tone: Technical, authoritative, data-backed, and direct.

# Output Format
Present the results in a Markdown table with the following headers:
| Angle | Hook (Under 125 chars) | Body Copy (1-2 short paragraphs) | Headline (Under 40 chars) | Button CTA |
```

## 2. CRO Landing Page Hero Section Architecture

A visitor decides whether to bounce within 3 seconds of landing on a page. The hero section must articulate the value proposition, the mechanism, and a friction-free call to action:

```markdown
# Role & Context
Act as an elite Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) strategist and UX copywriter trained in the Fogg Behavior Model and Cialdini's persuasion principles.

# Product Information
- Offer: Comprehensive Technical SEO & Core Web Vitals Audit for E-Commerce Stores
- Target Persona: Shopify Plus brand owners doing $2M–$20M in annual GMV.
- Primary Friction Point: "Audits are usually generic automated PDF exports with no actionable engineering execution."

# Task
Execute a 2-step process:
Step 1: Write an internal psychological breakdown (3 bullets) diagnosing why typical audit landing pages fail to convert enterprise e-commerce merchants.
Step 2: Produce a CRO-optimized Landing Page Hero section based on that breakdown.

# Output Structure
1. Main Headline (H1): Maximum 9 words. Benefit-driven with an explicit target audience anchor.
2. Subheadline (H2): 2 sentences. Clarifies the specific technical mechanism and neutralizes the primary friction point.
3. Primary Call-to-Action (CTA): High-intent, first-person active verb (e.g. "Claim My Technical Audit").
4. Friction-Neutralizing Microcopy: Placed immediately beneath the CTA button (e.g. "Takes 60 seconds • No credit card required • Reviewed by a Staff Engineer").
5. Above-the-Fold Social Proof Pill: Short 1-line trust badge referencing audited GMV or client logos.
```

## 3. Brand Voice Calibration via Few-Shot Exemplars

Large language models default to a standardized, generic corporate voice. To capture your agency's authentic voice, calibrate the model using **Few-Shot Exemplar Anchoring**:

```markdown
# Objective
Calibrate your writing style to match the exact brand voice, cadence, and vocabulary of Nexium Media.

# Golden Voice Exemplars (Analyze These Carefully)
<exemplar_1>
"Most marketing agencies sell you pretty mockups. We sell you pipeline velocity. If your landing page takes 3.8 seconds to load on mobile, you aren't running marketing campaigns—you're subsidizing Google's ad revenue with zero return."
</exemplar_1>

<exemplar_2>
"Zero-downtime rollouts aren't a luxury feature; they're the baseline standard of modern software engineering. If deploying a schema update requires an offline maintenance window in 2026, your architecture is broken."
</exemplar_2>

<exemplar_3>
"We don't do 80-page discovery decks that gather digital dust. We write code, test hypotheses against live traffic, and optimize for conversion rate lifts you can verify in Stripe."
</exemplar_3>

# Task
Analyze the sentence length distribution, vocabulary preferences (concise, technical, punchy), and conversational stance of the exemplars above.
Then, rewrite the following raw draft into this exact brand voice:

<raw_draft>
Our agency is dedicated to helping businesses grow by providing top-notch digital marketing solutions and exceptional web design services that will transform your online presence and unlock new potentials.
</raw_draft>
```

## 4. Email Nurturing Sequence (Objection Elimination)

```markdown
Generate a 3-part automated B2B email sequence targeted at enterprise leads who downloaded our "2026 Core Web Vitals Playbook" but have not yet booked an architectural consultation.

- Email 1 (Day 1): The High-Value Nugget. Deliver immediate utility, isolate the single biggest mistake merchants make with image optimization, and provide an actionable fix.
- Email 2 (Day 3): The Case Study Breakdown. Contrast a client who migrated from a legacy monolith to Next.js (metric: bounce rate dropped from 68% to 29%, average order value increased 18%).
- Email 3 (Day 5): The Direct Objection Breaker. Directly address the cost and complexity of a platform migration. Offer a free 20-minute architectural teardown call.

Constraints:
- Subject lines: Under 45 characters. No emojis. Curiosity or benefit driven.
- Reading level: 7th–8th grade (high readability, short sentences).
- End every email with a single, clear question rather than an aggressive hard pitch.
```

## Banned AI Vocabulary Checklist

Ensure every marketing prompt includes the following negative constraint block to maintain editorial authenticity:

```text
Never use any of the following overused AI marketing buzzwords:
- game-changer / revolutionary / disruptive
- unleash / unlock / skyrocket / supercharge
- delve / dive deep / navigate / realm / tapestry
- bespoke / seamless / holistic / synergy
- in today's fast-paced digital landscape
```
