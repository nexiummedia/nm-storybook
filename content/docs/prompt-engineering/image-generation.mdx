# Image Generation Prompt Engineering

Generative image synthesis has advanced rapidly with the release of **Midjourney v6.1**, **FLUX.1 (Dev / Schnell / Pro)**, and **DALL-E 3**. The obsolete habit of stuffing prompts with comma-separated keyword soup (e.g. `hyperrealistic, 8k resolution, octane render, trending on artstation, masterpiece`) is actively detrimental to modern transformer and flow-matching diffusion models.

Modern image engines are trained on natural language captions and understand **spatial relationships, cinematic lighting physics, lens optics, and typography**.

## The 5-Part Visual Prompt Architecture

Every high-fidelity image prompt generated at Nexium Media follows a 5-layer physical specification:

```text
1. Primary Subject & Action: The central entity, pose, wardrobe, expressions, and physical interaction.
2. Setting & Spatial Environment: Background elements, architectural style, atmospheric weather, depth planes.
3. Lighting Physics & Mood: Directional sources, color temperature (warm golden hour vs cool tungsten), specular highlights, shadows.
4. Camera Optics & Medium: Sensor size, focal length, aperture (depth of field), film stock, or digital grading.
5. Parameter Controls (CLI flags): Aspect ratio, stylization strength, model version, raw mode.
```

## Technical Camera & Lighting Glossary

Instead of vague terms like "high quality", use the physical terminology of professional cinematographers:

### Focal Lengths & Perspectives
- **`24mm / 28mm Wide Angle`**: Expansive perspective, deep depth of field, dramatic architectural and environmental context.
- **`35mm Lens`**: Natural documentary and photojournalistic perspective, minimal distortion, classic street photography aesthetic.
- **`50mm "Nifty Fifty"`**: True human eye perspective, versatile editorial portraits, balanced foreground/background relationship.
- **`85mm / 105mm Portrait Lens (f/1.4)`**: Shallow depth of field, creamy background blur (bokeh), flattering facial compression with zero perspective distortion.
- **`100mm Macro`**: Microscopic surface detail, water droplets, mechanical gears, fabric weave, extreme close-up.

### Lighting Styles
- **`Volumetric Lighting / God Rays`**: Beams of light cutting through haze, mist, or dust particles, creating tangible atmospheric depth.
- **`Chiaroscuro / Rim Lighting`**: High-contrast illumination inspired by Caravaggio, featuring a strong edge backlight that separates dark subjects from deep backgrounds.
- **`Golden Hour (3200K)`**: Low-angle, warm, amber directional sunlight casting long soft shadows.
- **`Diffused Overcast Softbox`**: Shadowless, flattering, even illumination ideal for commercial tech hardware and modern UI presentation.

## Midjourney v6.1 Parameter Reference Guide

| Parameter | Function | Recommended Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`--ar <ratio>`** | Controls the aspect ratio of the generated canvas. | `--ar 16:9` (Hero/Banner), `--ar 1:1` (Square/Avatar), `--ar 4:5` (Instagram/Social). |
| **`--v 6.1`** | Invokes Midjourney's latest image generation model. | `--v 6.1` (Always use latest for superior skin, hands, and coherence). |
| **`--style raw`** | Strips away Midjourney's default hyper-saturated aesthetic bias in favor of authentic photographic fidelity. | Essential for photorealistic, documentary, or commercial product shoots. |
| **`--stylize <0-1000>`** | Dictates the intensity of Midjourney's artistic flair. Lower values adhere more strictly to the prompt text. | Default is `100`. Use `50–150` for strict realism; `250–500` for stylized visual art. |
| **`--sref <URL>`** | Style Reference: Transfers the color palette, artistic texture, and mood of an existing reference image. | Ideal for maintaining brand consistency across an entire marketing campaign. |
| **`--cw <0-100>`** | Character Weight: When using `--cref` (Character Reference), dictates whether to copy face only (`--cw 0`) or entire outfit and body (`--cw 100`). | Use `--cw 0` when generating the same persona across different outfits or contexts. |

## Rendering Text Inside Images

To generate text accurately inside images:
- **Midjourney v6.1**: Enclose the exact words in double quotation marks: `A neon sign that reads "OPEN 24/7"`. Keep text under 4–5 words for maximum fidelity.
- **FLUX.1**: Describe the placement, medium, and lettering style explicitly: `A vintage cardboard box with the words "DELIVERY EXPRESS" stenciled in bold black industrial sans-serif lettering across the center panel.`
