# Delivery & Engineering Processes

Our processes are designed to deliver client websites on schedule with zero visual defects and exceptional search engine rankings.

## Core Workflows

- **[Development Workflow](/docs/processes/development-workflow)**: Branch naming, commit standards, pull requests, and peer reviews.
- **[Deployment & CI/CD](/docs/processes/deployment)**: Automated Vercel preview builds, SEO checklist verification, and DNS rollouts.

## Quality Assurance & Client Sign-Off

Before handing off any website to a client:
1. **Lighthouse Audit**: Mobile score >= 90 in Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO.
2. **Cross-Browser Verification**: Tested on Chrome, Safari (iOS), Firefox, and Samsung Internet.
3. **Form & Lead Testing**: Verification of inquiry form submissions, email notifications, and CRM capture.
4. **Client Review Window**: 48-hour staging review before production domain cutover.
