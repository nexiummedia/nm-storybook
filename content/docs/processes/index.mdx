# Engineering Processes

Our development processes are engineered to enable fast shipping while maintaining rock-solid platform stability.

## Core Workflows

- **[Development Workflow](/docs/processes/development-workflow)**: Branch naming, commit formats, pull request standards, and peer review expectations.
- **[Deployment & Release](/docs/processes/deployment)**: CI/CD automated test pipelines, canary releases, and zero-downtime rollbacks.

## Incident Management

We practice blameless post-mortems for any production incident severity P1 or P2. All incidents are logged in the engineering status portal within 24 hours of mitigation.
