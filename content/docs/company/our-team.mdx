# Our Team

Acme operates on a squad-based organizational model designed for autonomy, cross-functional alignment, and rapid feedback loops.

## Organizational Structure

Our teams are organized into autonomous, mission-driven units known as **Pods**:

```
Engineering Organization
├── Platform Foundation Pod
│   ├── Infrastructure & SRE
│   ├── Security & Compliance
│   └── Database Architecture
├── Core Product Pod
│   ├── Frontend Web & UI Library
│   ├── Backend Microservices
│   └── Mobile Applications
└── Developer Experience Pod
    ├── Documentation & SDKs
    ├── CI/CD & Tooling
    └── Public API Gateway
```

## Department Responsibilities

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Engineering & Infrastructure">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product & Design">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Security & Compliance">
    Ensures SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance. Conducts automated vulnerability scans, secret audits, and pen-testing drills.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customer Success & Support">
    Partners with enterprise customers to solve implementation questions, manages incident escalation, and writes comprehensive support guides.
  </Accordion>
