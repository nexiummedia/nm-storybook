# Our Team

Nexium Media operates with dedicated pods across web engineering, performance marketing, design, and client success.

## Organizational Structure

```
Nexium Media Organization
├── Web Engineering & Technology
│   ├── Next.js & React Frontend
│   ├── Node.js, Database & Cloud Hosting
│   └── Performance & Core Web Vitals
├── Performance Marketing & SEO
│   ├── Meta & Google Ads Management
│   ├── Local SEO & Google Business Profile
│   └── Marketing Automation & WhatsApp CRM
└── Creative & Client Strategy
    ├── UI/UX Brand Design & Prototyping
    ├── Copywriting & Conversion Optimization
    └── Client Success & Account Delivery
```

## Department Responsibilities

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Web Engineering & Frontend">
    Responsible for crafting blazing-fast Next.js and React web applications, custom CMS integrations, Razorpay checkout flows, and zero-downtime Vercel/AWS deployments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Performance Marketing & SEO">
    Manages high-ROI Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Search ad campaigns, keyword research, on-page SEO, backlink strategy, and local map pack rankings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Design & Creative Direction">
    Develops striking brand identities, conversion-focused landing page wireframes in Figma, ad creatives, and high-engagement social media assets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Client Success & Support">
    Direct point of contact for client communication, monthly ROI reviews, website updates, domain/DNS management, and technical troubleshooting.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
