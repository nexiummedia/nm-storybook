# Vision and Mission

At Acme, our daily efforts are guided by a commitment to simplify complex technology while fostering transparency and engineering excellence.

## Our Mission

> To accelerate human innovation by removing friction from the modern software development lifecycle.

We build platforms and tools that allow engineers to focus entirely on creative problem solving, while repetitive infrastructure tasks are automated away safely and predictably.

## Our Vision

By 2030, Acme aims to be the universal standard for enterprise cloud orchestration and internal documentation, connecting over 100,000 engineering teams globally.

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Radical Transparency
We default to public communication inside our organization. Internal documentation, architecture decisions, and roadmap prioritization are accessible to everyone across all departments.

### 2. High Agency & Ownership
Every engineer at Acme owns their code end-to-end—from initial design and automated testing to deployment and production monitoring.

### 3. Craftsmanship and Speed
We do not believe speed and quality are opposites. By investing early in sound architecture, robust design systems, and rigorous CI/CD test automation, we achieve rapid iterations with zero regression bugs.

<Callout type="warn">
Never sacrifice security or accessibility for the sake of release deadlines. If a timeline pressure arises, adjust the scope rather than compromise standards.
</Callout>
